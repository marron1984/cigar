Fundamentals

Basics

What a cigar is, how it is built, how to smoke one, the vocabulary of flavour, wrapper shades, history and etiquette — everything worth reading before your first cigar.

By Origin

Origins

A cigar's character is decided by the climate and soil where it was grown. Ten major origins, covered in depth — geography, history, cultivation and flavour, with sources.

By Size & Ring Gauge

By size

Ring gauge and vitola change both the flavour and how long a cigar lasts. Compare the standard sizes side by side.

By Price

By price

From dry cigars at the convenience store to bottles-of-wine money. What each price band gives you, which brands define it, and how to choose.

Accessories

Tools & storage

Cutters, lighters, humidors, ashtrays — how to choose and use the tools that keep a cigar at its best.

The Humidor · In depth

The Complete Humidor

A dedicated study of the humidor — the one piece of equipment that decides whether a cigar is worth smoking. Its history, the makers and brands worldwide, use and maintenance, types, and price bands, researched from five angles.

For the Connoisseur

Advanced — for the obsessed

Leaf varieties and priming, fermentation and the roller's craft, how to read a Cuban, the great houses, the finer points of smoking, pairing, and the science of storage and ageing. Past this point lies the deep end.

Doctorate · Research

Doctorate — the scholarship of cigars

Chemistry, combustion, botany, agronomy, sensory science, industrial economics, geopolitics and health science. A top-tier reference compiled from academic and specialist sources worldwide. This is the deepest part of the site. The openingliterature reviewworks through the peer-reviewed papers and official monographs in each field at length.

Japan Guide · Cigars in Japan

Japan Guide

A complete guide to buying and enjoying cigars in Japan: shops and cigar bars across all 47 prefectures, plus the country's history, taxes, smoking rules, Japanese etiquette and how to begin. Details change often — please check with each venue before visiting.

The World of Cigars

The World — everything cigar

Ten domains researched across sources worldwide: a full timeline, cultures and famous venues, notable aficionados, the Japan guide, tasting notes in practice, other kinds of cigars, investment and collecting, a multilingual glossary, a travel and buying guide, and the myths and FAQ.

The Marcas · Brand encyclopedia

Brands

An encyclopedia of the world's cigar brands (marcas), each with its history from founding, the origin of its name, and its signature vitolas — beginning with every Cuban marca.
